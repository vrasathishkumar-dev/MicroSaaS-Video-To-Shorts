# Playbook · Growth Loop (weekly)

Runs every week once the product is usable. Owned by `09-digital-marketing`.

## Monday — pick one bottleneck

From the funnel, name the single worst leak:

```text
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Trial → Paid → Retained → Referred
```

If nobody arrives, content and channel work.
If they arrive and leave, the landing page or the promise is wrong.
If they sign up and never activate, it is a product problem, not a marketing one.
**Do not run marketing on a product with an activation problem.** Send it to PM.

## Tuesday-Thursday — run exactly two experiments

```text
EXPERIMENT
Bottleneck:  activation
Change:      add a 3-step onboarding checklist
Hypothesis:  users who see the checklist reach the core action more often
Measure:     % reaching core action within 24h
Pass line:   +10 percentage points, minimum 30 users
Cost:        ₹0
Owner:       vc-engineer
```

Two. Not five. With this sample size, more experiments means less learning.

## Friday — growth review

`21-data-analyst` provides the numbers, `09-digital-marketing` writes the verdict:

```text
Channel | actions | leading metric | conversions | verdict
SEO     | 2 posts | 40 impressions | 0            | KEEP (too early)
LinkedIn| 5 posts | 1,200 views    | 3 signups    | DOUBLE DOWN
Reddit  | 4 comments | 90 clicks   | 0 signups    | FIX (wrong subreddits)
```

Verdicts: `DOUBLE DOWN / KEEP / FIX / STOP`.

**Stopping a channel is normal and good.** A channel with no conversions after
4 weeks of honest effort gets STOP, and the effort moves to the one that works.

## The two rules that matter most

1. **Never run more than two channels at once.** A one-person company running
   five channels is running none of them.
2. **"TOO SMALL TO TELL" is usually the right verdict.** At 37 signups, a 5%
   difference is noise. Say so instead of manufacturing a conclusion.

## Compounding vs linear

Every quarter, check the mix. Linear channels (outreach, manual posting) buy
time. Compounding channels (SEO, content, referral, integrations) buy freedom —
and freedom is the actual goal of this company. If everything on the list is
linear, the company is a job, not a business. Say that out loud in the board
review.
